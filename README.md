- Wander around, play, and interact with Hololive talents in this mythical world hosted by Ninomae Ina'nis, or just relax and enjoy a seemingly alive and peaceful(?) world.
## Core Systems:
<!-- ![talents interacting]() -->
<!-- what systems/features this game will have -->
### Entity Simulation
<!-- i could really go for some cookies right now -->
- Myth: Hololive talents engage in activities and conversations, drawing, playing, and living a life of their own.
- Takodachi: With the help of Tentacult (not a cult!) voicing the creatures of the void, takodachis come in many shapes and forms. Some tend to group and spend time by the pond, while others just look for a nice shade to take a nap or enjoy some cookies. Takos are mostly independent, but they will ask Ina for cookies when hungry.
### World Interactions
<!-- touch this, touch that -->
- There are many activities spread around the void, like the pond where you have the pedal boat and, of course, a fishing minigame.
### Talent Interactions
<!-- can't touch this -->
- Talents will behave according to their own personalities, and this defines what they like to do in the void.
- You can play and chat with talents to your heart's content, as long as you don't annoy them~ but if you annoy Ina too much, she may get the crowbar, yeet you far away, or squish you into oblivion.

## World
### The Void
<!-- ![an overview of the void]() -->
- The fog covers most of it, but it appears to be built on a small island. There's a circular perimeter wall standing tall with two big gates to the outside, but they're locked.
- It resembles a traditional Japanese sanctuary, and at the center stands Ina's studio, with the flora mixing in with the buildings.
### The Studio
<!-- ![entrance of the studio]() -->
- Inside has everything Ina could need to create art or study the art of gaming… And please leave your shoes at the entrance before going onto the main floor!
### The Mural
- Just beside the studio, there's a mural filled with pictures, drawings, and messages from Tentacult from around the world. ~~A clear indicator of progress towards world domination.~~
### The Zen Garden
<!-- ![talents drawing in the sand]() -->
- Here, talents and takodachis can draw and write on the sand or stack rock piles. It stands near the mural, and there's a rock pathway that leads to an ominous shrine.
### The Shrine
<!-- ![ao-chan hovering]() -->
- It's an ominous-looking altar consisting of five pillars. There's a different symbol on each of the pillars, and the middle one has AO-chan hovering above it. It emits a faint beam of light.
### The Playground
<!-- ![takodachis playing around]() -->
- Who hasn't wished they didn't have to grow up? This is where everyone brings back their inner child and just enjoys the current moment without a single worry.
### The Pond
<!-- ![the pond]() -->
- Just behind the studio, you can find the pond with a dock that leads to a pedal boat. To the side there's a nice tree with a picnic blanket underneath. There's a stream coming from the side, which fills the pond and overflows on the opposite side, going into the void.
### The Giant Tree
<!-- ![an old looking tree]() -->
- A very big tree, with just a few leaves and flowers left. There are colored lamps hanging on different branches, some of the lights are out.
### The Maze Garden
<!-- ![a towering and difficult maze... for takos]() -->
- A flower maze garden that zigs and zags.
### The Open Theater
<!-- ![hopefully, the 'open' in 'open theater' means it's free]() -->
- A half-circle stage where talents can do concerts and theatrical plays.
### The Outside
<!-- ![tentacles]() -->
- The outside is kind of scary... let's just not talk about it, okay?
