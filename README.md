### About the project:
- This game was born from some tests for another game but ended up becoming its own thing. Wah~ndering takos is about simulating entities' behaviors and needs, while the player navigates around and interacts with them and the environment, the aim is to have natural interactions and dynamic events happening around the player, which the player can take part in or simply enjoy what's happening. there are also many references and hidden lore sprinkled around the game world that can be discovered for those who like to poke around more.
- Why involve hololive? It's a motivator for me. My first experience with Vtubers was in 2020 by watching clips of Hololive Myth, and 2020 was such a bad year for mental health, watching them really helped me get through it. That's something that stuck with me. Two weeks before Gura's graduation I was gifted a membership from the community, this account was created a few days after the graduation as my gift to the community.

## Core Vision:
<!-- ![a glimpse of the world]() -->
- Wander around, play and interact with Hololive talents in this mythical world hosted by Ninomae Ina'nis or just relax and enjoy a seemingly alive and peaceful(?) world.

## Core Systems:
### Autonomous Characters
<!-- ![talents interacting]() -->
- Myth: Hololive talents engage in conversations, play games, make drawings and do many other activities, living a life of their own.
<!-- ![tentacult contributions]() -->
- Takodachi: With the help of Tentacult (not a cult!) voicing the creatures of the void, takodachis come in many shapes and forms, some tend to group and spend time by the pond, others are just looking for a nice shade to take a nap or enjoy the taste of cookies. Takos are mostly independent, but they will ask Ina for cookies when hungry.
### Talent Motivations
<!-- ![ina yeet]() -->
- Talents will behave according to their own personalities and motivations and this ultimately defines what they like to do in the void, they will invite the player and other talents to various interactions around the world and have a damn good day, but if you, for example, annoy Ina too much, she may yeet you far away or squish you into oblivion.
### World Interactions
<!-- ![tako interacting]() -->
- There are many interactive objects and mini-games, plus a few hidden events sprinkled around the void. Feel free to discover all of them, or just find a cozy spot to relax~
## World
### The Void
<!-- ![an overview of the void]() -->
- The dimension from which many eldritch tales come from. One of those tales is about an ordinary young girl that once stumbled upon an old book, giving her knowledge of things most couldn't even fathom, but that's a story for another time.
- It's a place where Ina can draw, relax or play to her heart's content, free of outside worries. A place to ease the mind. Ease her mind, to be more precise, as most wouldn't really consider the void as being the most peaceful of places…
### The Studio
<!-- ![entrace of the studio]() -->
- It's the main building, this is where Ina is supposed to focus on her work, but instead she spends more time on the floor or drawing. Please leave your shoes at the entrance before going into the main floor! Inside has everything Ina could need to create art or study the art of gaming…
### The Mural
<!-- ![tentacult contributions]() -->
- Just beside the studio, there's a mural filled with pictures, drawings, and messages from tentacult from around the world. ~~A clear indicator of progress towards world domination.~~
### The Zen garden
<!-- ![talents drawing in the sand]() -->
- Here, talents and takodachis can draw and write on the sand or stack rock piles. It stands near the mural and there's a rock pathway that leads to an ominous shrine.
### The Shrine
<!-- ![ao-chan hovering]() -->
- It's an ominous-looking altar, consisting of five pillars. There's a different symbol on each of the pillars, and the middle one has AO-chan hovering above it. It emits a faint beam of light, like it's projecting something, but it's not really clear. Why don't you ask Ina more about it?
### The Playground
<!-- ![takodachis playing around]() -->
- Who hasn't ever wished they didn't have to grow up? This is where everyone brings back their inner child and just enjoys the current moment without a single worry.
### The Pond
<!-- ![a takodachi pov of the pond]() -->
- Just behind the studio, you can find the pond with a dock that leads to a pedal boat. To the side there's a nice tree with a picnic blanket underneath. There's a stream coming from the side, which fills the pond and overflows on the opposite side, the stream passes under a small bridge and goes out into the void. Some spots are filled with cattails and water lilies. Tiny takos like to spend time in the safety of the pond, the way they move resemble frogs.
### The Giant Tree
<!-- ![an old looking tree]() -->
- A very big tree, with just a few leaves and flowers left. There are colored lamps hanging on different branches, they have decals that resemble the talents, some of the lights are out.
### The Maze Garden
<!-- ![a towering and difficult maze... for takos]() -->
- A flower maze garden that zigs and zags 
### The Open Theater
<!-- ![hopefully, the 'open' in 'open theater' means it's free]() -->
- A half-circle stage where talents can do concerts and theatrical plays.
### The Outside
<!-- ![tentacles]() -->
- The outside is kind of scary... let's just not talk about it, okay?
